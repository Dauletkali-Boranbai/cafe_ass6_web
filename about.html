Our Story & Concept

Satti Cafe is a modern restaurant offering Eastern, Kazakh, Japanese and European cuisine. We are located at Mangilik El 16/1 in Astana.

Our cafe is a comfortable place for family dinners, meetings with friends and business lunches.

BOZA Brewery

One of the special features of Satti Cafe is our BOZA brewery. Guests can enjoy our drinks with their meals.

Cozy Summer Terrace

During the warm season, guests can relax on our summer terrace and enjoy their time with friends.
